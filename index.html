02 — ABOUT THE ARTIST

The Person Behind the Presence

“Every Story Begins With a Voice.”

Abdul Umar Sahan Nethmina is a multidisciplinary artist whose work bridges the contemplative intimacy of literature, the captivating resonance of public oratory, and the raw vulnerability of vocal music.

Rejecting the artificial boundaries of a single discipline, Sahan approaches every creation as an excavation of truth. As a novel writer, his prose dissects the delicate architecture of human memory, spiritual solitude, and cultural identity. As a speaker, he commands stages with a quiet intensity that dismantles pretension and connects strangers in shared introspection. And as a singer, his melodic arrangements give voice to emotions that evade prose.

Based in Sri Lanka with a global outlook, his journey is defined by one relentless conviction: that art must neither flatter the ego nor merely entertain, but rather awaken the forgotten depths within every listener and reader.

Through his independent brand and creative label, AbdulFandom (abdulfandom.lk), headquartered in Colombo, Sahan extends this ethos into physical reality—publishing author editions, creating signature heavyweight apparel, and designing artisanal desk artifacts for intentional living.
